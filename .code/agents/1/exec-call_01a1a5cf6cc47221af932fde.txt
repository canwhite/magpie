Each key of a remote-magpie provider also gets a card of its own, under `<remote>/~keys`, one section per key (#1547, `remoteKeyCards` in `remote_key_limits.go`): the gateway key's limit as that magpie holds it, read from `GET /v1/magpie/limit` asked with that key alone, so a key never sees another's numbers. It shows a window per cap (tokens, spend) named for the period, used against the cap, with its reset, plus `GatewayKey` (what is left, calls, a cycle of days, used up); a key held to no limit shows `Unlimited`. It is kept for `remoteCardsFor` like the remote's cards and read again by the card's refresh (`?provider=<remote>/~keys&user=<key name>`). A read that fails keeps the last numbers, dated (`AsOf`); with none the card says why, never zero or no limit. A remote from before `/v1/magpie/limit` (404) says it is too old to tell (`errRemoteNoKeyLimit`). A remote that holds the key to a budget, models or accounts answers its cards 403 `gateway_key_held` (#1981, `errKeyHeld`): the cards kept from before are dropped and only the key's own card shows. Its owner can let the key be told the cards of what it may use (#2216, `magpie gateway-key quotas <id> on`); the remote then answers those, and 403 as before when that is none. A remote's cards are kept by provider and key (`remoteCardsID`), so a changed key never shows what the old one was told, and a read is kept only over one asked before it (`keepRemoteCards`). Cursor, Grok and a Kiro key keep the page's cards only. With Settings → Allowances → *Allowance reads* at *When I ask* (`QuotaReads == "asked"`, #1518: magpie started at login before the proxy app read by itself, direct), only a read the user asked for goes out: the Usage page or the tray panel's Allowances opened (no more than once in 30s, `quotasOpened`, #1588) or refreshed (`?asked=1`), a card's refresh (`RefreshUsage`), `magpie quota`, `magpie accounts`, `magpie quota wait` and the TUI's quotas (`Asked`). Any other read (`quotaReading` → `holdUnasked`) carries `netproxy.Hold`, so its requests fail before they leave, and every reader gives back what it last read, dated, or "not read yet" (`errNotAsked`): routing, the used-up switch, the menu bar and alerts go by that. An account refused for its quota (`StaleAllowance` → `markStaleRead`) or a key refused (`StaleKeyAllowance` → `refused`) is still read, once (`unheld`).
